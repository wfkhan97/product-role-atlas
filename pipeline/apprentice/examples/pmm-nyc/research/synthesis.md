# Synthesis: what is Agentio betting on that nobody has shown working yet?

Agentio is betting that creator ads can be bought and measured with the rigor of Meta or Google ads: priced on expected
views, amortized over ninety days, attributed through pixels, post-purchase surveys and marketing-mix models, and
allocated by models that read the content itself. The evidence for the bet is real but company-reported: a 5.3x ROAS at
Bombas, 81% higher ROAS in the Meta beta, more than 100 enterprise brands in 2025, and $40M from Forerunner on the
thesis that this is infrastructure, not an agency. What nobody has shown in public is the independent half of the
claim: no outside study of Agentio's ROAS exists, the pricing model's accuracy has never been benchmarked, the
"hundreds of thousands of pieces of content" claim appears in the posting and nowhere else, and the brands moving
budget at Unilever's scale are not yet on the platform. The marketing team is being hired now (the Narrative PMM is the
second product-marketing hire; the Product Marketing Lead posting asks for "conservative-but-confident claims that
sophisticated audiences trust"), which says the company knows the gap is in the telling as much as in the proof. The
job, then, is to write the story a performance marketer believes before the independent proof exists, without a claim
that will not survive it. Every card on the map aims at that gap.

**One line:** Agentio is betting that creator ads can be bought and measured like Meta or Google ads, and no one outside
the company has yet shown that its numbers hold; the job is to tell that story in words a performance marketer trusts
before the proof is public.

# Agentio: how it talks about the Narrative PMM role and the marketing team

## The posting, in its own words

"Product Marketing Manager, Narrative", Marketing, New York, NY, published 2026-10-20 on Agentio's Ashby board; no compensation field is published [1][2]. The role is a writer: "You are the storyteller for Agentio's product"; "You take the facts, stats and launch briefs the product marketing team produces and turn them into the words, decks and one-pagers"; "If a customer has an aha moment reading our pitch, you wrote the sentence" [1].

The second half is maintenance: "You also own the enablement system. Every deck, battlecard and playbook stays current when anything changes"; "Sales and CS never present stale material" [1]. The working model is a hand-off from a sibling PMM: "You work alongside a Product Marketing Manager who runs launches and owns the data. They hand you a brief"; "You decide how to say them. Same model as a brand briefing a creator" [1].

Ownership bullets (verbatim): "The overarching Agentio story: what we offer, how it works, why customers love it"; "The aha layer: value prop language per persona, modular story blocks, emotional copy"; "The modular pitch by SKU and brand type"; "Pitch deck, one-pagers, battlecards, QBR template, measurement guidance and onboarding playbook, each updated within a week of a launch brief"; "one asset library, every asset current whenever product, pricing or packaging changes"; "Weekly collateral requests from sales and CS"; "Win/loss language folded back into battlecards"; "Customer-facing framing for launch emails, LinkedIn posts and website product pages" [1]. There is no "What You Need" or years-of-experience section [1].

The company story to retell: "buy creator led ads as easily and scalably as Meta or Google ads", a "$800B digital ad ecosystem", customers "Uber, DoorDash, and Bombas", "$56M at a $340M valuation, including a Series B led by Forerunner", Forbes Next Billion Dollar Startups 2025, Inc. Best Places to Work 2026 "On the Rise: 0–4 Years in Business" [1].

## The marketing and GTM team from the sibling postings

The board lists 26 open roles, all New York; six are Marketing (Product Marketing Lead, Growth Marketing Lead, Brand Communications & Events Lead, Brand Designer, Video Content Producer, Creator in Residence), and Marketing Science Lead sits under Sales [2].

Product Marketing Lead is "the first Product Marketing Lead on the team", "Reporting to the VP, Marketing", and asks for "conservative-but-confident claims that sophisticated audiences trust", to "Ship the full enablement bundle", and to "eliminate ad-hoc decks and inconsistent messaging"; its launch-calendar and KPI remit matches the Narrative role's brief-giver [3]. Growth Marketing Lead builds demand gen "aligned to the product narrative and ICP" and is "a natural collaborator with Sales, Content, and PMM" [4]. Brand Communications & Events Lead will "Craft press-worthy storylines from product narratives" and must grasp "the reputational stakes of inaccurate claims in ad-tech and AI" [5]. Brand Designer is "its first brand design hire", reporting "directly to our VP of Marketing" [6]. Marketing Science Lead builds "the 'performance-grade pipes'" (MMM, lift tests, attribution) [7]. Sales Director must "deliver a clear and concise pitch about the value that Agentio will bring to marketers" [8]. Creator in Residence will "Help package case studies and campaign proof points into content people actually want to watch" [9].

Repeated across the marketing postings: "A deep obsession with using AI tools and LLMs to gain 10x leverage", "work harder than you've likely ever worked before, but with far greater reward", "in-office 5 days/week" [3][4][5]. The VP, Marketing appears by title only; no public page naming the person was found [3][6].

## Culture and how they hire

The careers page lists six values: "Build the Future, Move at Unprecedented Speed", "Obsess Over Impact", "Run Through Walls", "Always Be Lore Maxxing", "Take Intelligent Risks, Learn Relentlessly", "No Ego, All In"; the team works in person from a NoMad office [10]. Inc.'s profile lists Agentio as a 2026 Inc. Best Workplaces honoree, "On the Rise" stage, 25–49 employees, cited for weekly gratitude practices and team-chosen monthly outings [11]. The Creator in Residence posting shows the hiring style: applicants send "Two series you would launch for Agentio in the first 90 days" [9].

## Public writing and talks by leadership

Arthur Leopold, "CEO & Co-Founder", and Jonathan Meyers, "CTO & Co-Founder", co-authored the Series B post: "We're hiring world-class engineers and GTM operators"; "Attention has already moved, and ad dollars always follow attention" [12]; TechCrunch confirms the titles [13]. Leopold on MarTech Series (2024-12-05): "We believe the future of advertising is creators telling a brand's story" [14]. On Retail Brew (2026-03-06): creators "translate your brand's message into their own voice"; "the real shift isn't a new metric; it's a new measurement philosophy" [15]. On Antler's Further, Faster (2026-03-10, host Jeff Becker): "If you starve your team, they will build a farm"; hire "the most resourceful, ambitious, hungry people" [16][17]. On The CPG Guys (2026-10-14): "the dollars will always follow attention if you make it easy"; "We are not commoditizing or programatizing the end content" [18]. The Inflection/26 CMO summit recap quotes guests only [19]. No Substack or marketing-leader bylines found.

## What a hiring manager here would forward

Work that mirrors the posting: an "aha" rewrite of a stats-heavy brief, a battlecard with win/loss language folded in, a one-library enablement system updated within a week of a launch, and "conservative-but-confident" claims, since inaccurate ad-tech claims are treated as reputational risk [1][3][5]. The brief-to-creator analogy and "dollars follow attention" are the frames leadership repeats [1][12][18].

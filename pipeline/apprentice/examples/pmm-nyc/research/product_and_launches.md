# Agentio: product and the last twelve months

## What the product does

Agentio sells itself as "The AI-Native Platform for Creator Advertising" and "AI infrastructure that scales human creativity and unlocks ambitious growth" [1]. The job-board boilerplate puts it more plainly: "Agentio enables marketers to buy creator led ads as easily and scalably as Meta or Google ads," with sourcing, pricing, contracting, delivery and measurement automated [2].

Concretely, the platform matches creators to brand goals and automates briefs, brand-safety checks, contracting, content approval, performance tracking, payments and mid-flight optimization; marketers can set up custom AI agents in natural language [3]. The home page says it runs on first-party creator audience and performance data and that "Every creator is verified, brand-safe, transparently priced" [1]. It is a closed marketplace: creators opt in and connect their channel by API, and Agentio reviews real audience data before admission [14].

Platforms: YouTube creator integrations (YouTube-only at launch [24]) and, since July 2026, Meta Partnership Ads on Instagram and Facebook; "across YouTube, Meta, and beyond" [1][6].

"AI-native" in their words: "AI-native describes a system where the intelligence sits underneath the product rather than on top of it" and "AI-native means the system learns from real transaction and performance data on every deal it runs" [11]. AI handles matching, briefing, measurement and optimization; humans set strategy and approve content, to "Collapse endless manual, transactional steps into 2 that actually matter" [1].

Measurement: pixels plus post-purchase surveys for creator-level ranking, marketing-mix modelling for channel-level budget decisions [16]; views are tracked over a 90-day window [11].

## Launches Oct 2025–Oct 2026 (newest first)

- **2026-10-07 — Fairing native integration.** Two-way integration that syncs the active creator roster into Fairing's post-purchase survey as named options and sends responses back with verified, deduplicated order IDs, so renewals can be judged on pixel plus survey revenue. Live now; no pricing stated. Quote: "Creators drive purchases pixels never see." [10]
- **2026-09-03 — Engaged-view pricing stated.** After YouTube's 24 Aug view-count change, Agentio reported about 60% of new views are engaged views (75,000+ videos) and said "every integration is priced on engaged views" [13].
- **2026-08-12 — "Unlocking an Infinite Creative Engine" report.** $130M across 65,000 Meta Partnership Ads and 137 brands: ~$1,000 test spend before keep/kill, winners fatigue after ~36 days, keeping 10 winners needs ~40 new creator ads a month [9][27].
- **2026-08-05 — Fast Company feature** ("The influencer bubble is finally popping"): Gruns runs its whole social ad program through Agentio, over a thousand ads live at once [21].
- **2026-07-27 — Inflection/26**, inaugural closed-door CMO summit, ~50 leaders [20].
- **2026-07-13 — Meta Partnership Ads, general availability.** "Creative Intelligence" scans every ad in a brand's Meta account, diagnoses fatigue and gaps, briefs and matches creators, and launches partnership ads on Instagram and Facebook within about a week; payment is performance-based, with no upfront fee to test and creators paid in proportion to spend on winning ads. Beta results (company-reported): 81% higher ROAS, 89% higher CTR, 13% lower CPA vs the same brands' non-Agentio creator ads [6][7][8]. Leopold: "Expanding to Meta's platforms is the next natural step for Agentio." [8]
- **2026-01-22 — "What AI-Native Actually Means" post** (definition above) [11].
- **2026-01-14 — 2026 YouTube Creator Marketing Playbook.** From 10,000+ integrations: ~40% of views arrive after day 30; CTR +10% per repeat integration [12].
- **2025-11-18 — $40M Series B** (see Funding) [3][4][26].

## Pricing and packaging

No public price list, SKUs or tiers [1][14]. Brands "control their own budget and pay for the media they buy" at CPMs set through bidding; YouTube integrations are auto-priced on expected first-30-day views, amortized over 90 days [14][15]. Meta ads are performance-paid, no upfront fee to test [6]. The demo form asks for monthly Meta spend in bands from under $250k to $5M+ [1]. Minimum spend: not found [14]. Self-serve vs managed: not stated; access is by demo request [1]. A 20% fee on brand spend, no cut from creators, "similar to The Trade Desk," was stated at the 2023 launch [23][24]; no current figure found.

## Customers and proof

Logos on the site include Warby Parker, Bombas, Away, Olipop, DoorDash, Uber, HelloFresh, Cash App, Mint Mobile, Vuori, WHOOP, Wayfair, Hims, Chime and Quince [1]. Over 100 enterprise brands ran campaigns in 2025 [3]. Case studies: Bombas (5.3x ROAS, 2x CAC efficiency, 90% net-new customers, 450+ integrations; featured by Think with Google, Apr 2026) [17][18]; Graza (YouTube + Meta, 33.3M views at 2.0x expected delivery, ~50% below contracted CPM) [19]; Turo (435 creators in a year); SURI (153 partnerships in ~5 months); Klover (approved 96% of AI matches); Maev; Outloud Talent [18][11]. Olipop's Steven Vigilante: "For the first time, we can scale this channel and measure the ROI on every dollar spent." [3]

## Funding

- Seed, 1 Nov 2023: $4.25M co-led by Craft Ventures and AlleyCorp, with Antler, Protagonist, Permanent Capital [24].
- Series A, 19 Nov 2024: $12M led by Benchmark, Craft and AlleyCorp participating [25].
- Series B, 18 Nov 2025: $40M led by Forerunner, with Benchmark, Craft Ventures, AlleyCorp; total raised $56M; valuation $340M; >5x YoY growth; Rhett & Link joined as advisors [3][4][26].

## What they say is next

Series B use of funds: expand past YouTube to Meta Partnership Ads "and additional platforms," grow from 35 to 100+ employees in NYC, scale internationally [3]. The July 2026 release says expansion across social platforms continues and headcount will double by end of 2026, naming no platform [6]; TikTok is not mentioned [8]. In 2023 the targets were Twitch, Discord and Snapchat [26], and CPA buying was "planned" [23]. On a March 2026 podcast the CEO (team of 35) framed the goal as closing the gap between creators' share of attention and share of ad spend, calling it a "DoubleClick moment" [22]. No dated public roadmap found.

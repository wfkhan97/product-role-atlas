## The role

**Product Marketing Manager** (`pmm`, field marketing): 609 postings from 332 employers. Startups 62%, large companies 38%, consulting, banks and funds 0%, other 0%.
What sets the role apart from other roles: Positioning & messaging, Go-to-market strategy.

## The skills, and what each means in this field

Core = asked by most postings (half or more); common = by many (a quarter or more). Share = postings that ask. Kind = how a person shows it (Craft: artefact; Working with people: scope; How you work: through; Technical: built; Domain: context; Tools: named; Credentials: named).

### Track · Go-to-market & business (`gtm`)

- **Positioning & messaging** (`positioning_messaging`, Craft, core, 95%, sets the role apart). Deciding how the product is described: to whom, against what alternative, and why it is better, in words customers repeat. In this field: The core craft: positioning statements, messaging hierarchies and the words of the brand.
- **Go-to-market strategy** (`gtm`, Craft, core, 87%, sets the role apart). Planning how a product reaches customers: channel, positioning, launch, enablement, and what success looks like commercially. In this field: The core: segment, message, channel and launch plan for every product and campaign.
- **Sales enablement & field partnership** (`sales_enablement`, Working with people, core, 69%). Giving sales and customer-facing teams what they need to sell and support the product: training, materials, and a feedback channel back.
- **Market & competitive analysis** (`market_research`, Craft, core, 64%). Understanding the market you are in: size, trends, competitors, and where the openings are. In this field: Market, competitor and customer research behind segmentation and positioning.
- **Growth, funnels & retention** (`growth_loops`, Craft, common, 36%). Growing usage systematically: acquisition, activation, retention, referral, and the loops that compound.
- **Partnerships & ecosystem** (`partnerships`, Working with people, common, 27%, asked more at senior levels). Building and managing relationships with other companies: integrations, platforms, resellers, and the ecosystem around the product. In this field: Co-marketing and agency partnerships that extend reach.
- **Pricing & packaging** (`pricing_packaging`, Craft, common, 25%, asked more at senior levels). Deciding what to charge, how to bundle features into tiers, and how the model shapes who buys and how they use the product.

### Track · Leadership & communication (`leadership`)

- **Cross-functional leadership** (`cross_functional`, Working with people, core, 76%). Leading a team you don't manage: engineering, design, data, marketing, sales, legal, all pulling toward one outcome. In this field: Working with product, sales and agencies so a launch or campaign is one coordinated effort.
- **Stakeholder management** (`stakeholder_mgmt`, Working with people, core, 59%). Keeping the people who can block or accelerate your work informed, heard, and on side: executives, partner teams, customers. In this field: Aligning sales, product and leadership behind one message and one launch plan.
- **Customer & executive-facing engagement** (`customer_facing_exec`, Working with people, core, 53%). Representing the product to the outside world: customer executives, advisory boards, conferences, analysts, and press.
- **Executive communication & reviews** (`exec_communication`, Working with people, common, 27%, asked more at senior levels). Communicating with executives and boards: the brief, the business review, and the ask, in the little time they have. In this field: Marketing reviews with leadership: results, the plan and the budget ask.

### Track · Domain knowledge (`domain`)

- **Enterprise SaaS & B2B** (`enterprise_saas`, Domain, core, 53%, asked more at senior levels). Selling software to companies: admin, security, procurement, multi-tenant platforms, and long sales cycles.

### Track · Technical fluency (`technical`)

- **AI / machine learning** (`ai_ml`, Technical, core, 74%, asked more at senior levels). Understanding how machine learning products work: data, models, evaluation, and the product decisions that differ from deterministic software. In this field: Personalization, targeting and content models that lift campaign performance.
- **Generative AI / LLMs & agents** (`genai_llm`, Technical, common, 40%, asked more at senior levels). Building products on large language models and agents: prompting, retrieval, evaluation, cost, safety, and the new UX patterns they need.
- **Technical fluency** (`technical_fluency`, Technical, common, 28%). Understanding how software is built well enough to make good trade-offs with engineers, without needing to write the code yourself.
- **Cloud platforms** (`cloud`, Technical, common, 26%, asked more at senior levels). Working knowledge of cloud platforms: core services, cost, and how infrastructure choices shape the product.

### Track · Product craft (`craft`)

- **Product launches** (`launch`, Craft, core, 61%). Getting a product out the door: readiness, staged rollout, enablement, announcement, and the follow-through once real users hit it.
- **Product strategy** (`product_strategy`, Craft, common, 45%, asked more at senior levels). Choosing where to play and how to win: which customers, which problems, which bets, and what you will deliberately not do.
- **Roadmapping** (`roadmap`, Craft, common, 42%, asked more at senior levels). Turning strategy into a sequenced, time-boxed plan of what the team will build, and keeping it honest as reality changes.
- **Prioritization & trade-offs** (`prioritization`, Craft, common, 30%, asked more at senior levels). Deciding what to do first, what to defer, and what to kill, with a rationale others can check, under constraints you don't control. In this field: Choosing channels, campaigns and segments for a fixed budget.

### Track · How you work (`behaviours`)

- **Written & verbal communication** (`communication`, How you work, core, 95%). Writing and speaking so that busy people understand, remember, and act: clear, short, structured, and matched to the audience. In this field: Positioning, messaging and campaign briefs: the words the market and the sales team repeat.
- **Ownership & bias for action** (`ownership`, How you work, core, 77%, asked more at senior levels). Treating outcomes as yours: driving work to done, taking responsibility when it goes wrong, and not waiting to be told. In this field: Owning a campaign or channel budget and the pipeline or revenue it must return.
- **Execution & delivery** (`execution`, How you work, core, 64%, asked more at senior levels). Getting things done reliably and well: driving to outcomes, holding quality, and keeping the details from slipping. In this field: Launching campaigns on the calendar and delivering the pipeline or revenue they promised.
- **Collaboration & teamwork** (`collaboration`, How you work, core, 63%). Being good to work with: generous with credit, open to being wrong, and reliable to the people around you. In this field: Working with sales, product, agencies and creative so one campaign carries one message.
- **Adaptability & fast-paced environments** (`adaptability`, How you work, core, 58%). Working well when the plan changes: ambiguity, pace, pivots, and growth that outruns process. In this field: Fast-moving channels and campaigns: learning and switching tactics as results come in.
- **Influence without authority** (`influence`, How you work, core, 53%, asked more at senior levels). Getting people to change course without being able to tell them to: evidence, framing, relationships, and timing. In this field: Getting sales, product and leadership to carry one positioning and fund the plan.
- **Customer obsession / user empathy** (`customer_obsession`, How you work, common, 35%). Making decisions from the customer's point of view, backed by direct contact with them, rather than from internal convenience.
- **Product sense & judgment** (`product_sense`, How you work, common, 32%, asked more at senior levels). Judgment about what makes a product good: spotting the right problem, the elegant solution, and the detail that will annoy users.
- **Structured problem solving** (`problem_solving`, How you work, common, 31%). Breaking an ambiguous situation into parts, finding the real cause, and choosing an approach when the answer isn't obvious. In this field: Diagnosing why a funnel, campaign or message underperforms and what to change.
- **Data-driven decision making** (`data_driven`, How you work, common, 29%). Using data to make and defend decisions: pulling it, questioning it, and knowing when it is wrong or insufficient. In this field: Measuring every channel and campaign and moving the budget on the results.

### Asked, outside the tracks

- **Content & social media marketing** (`content_social`, Craft, common, 38%, area Marketing & brand). Planning and producing content (blogs, video, social posts) that draws and engages an audience.
- **Marketing campaigns** (`campaign_management`, Craft, common, 35%, area Marketing & brand). Planning, launching and optimizing marketing campaigns across channels.
- **Program & project management** (`program_mgmt`, Working with people, common, 31%, area Delivery & process). Coordinating large, multi-team efforts: plans, milestones, dependencies, status, and getting the whole thing to land.
- **Metrics & KPIs** (`metrics_kpis`, Craft, common, 31%, area Data & experimentation). Choosing the few numbers that tell you whether the product is working, instrumenting them, and watching them with discipline.
- **AI tooling in daily work** (`ai_tools`, Tools, common, 30%, area Tools). Using AI tools in daily product work: prototyping, analysis, writing, and research, faster than before.
- **Personas & segmentation** (`personas_jtbd`, Craft, common, 41%, area Discovery & research). Describing who you are building for in a way that is specific enough to change decisions: segments, personas, and the jobs they hire the product for.
- **Customer stories & advocacy** (`customer_proof`, Craft, common, 37%, area Marketing & brand). Turning customer wins into case studies, references and advocacy that help sell.
- **Process design & operational rigor** (`process_improvement`, Craft, common, 32%, area Delivery & process). Designing how work gets done so it scales: operating cadence, mechanisms, playbooks, and removing waste.

## About the Role

You are the storyteller for Agentio's product. You take the facts, stats and launch briefs the product marketing team produces and turn them into the words, decks and one-pagers brands and our GTM team use every day. If a customer has an aha moment reading our pitch, you wrote the sentence.

You also own the enablement system. Every deck, battlecard and playbook stays current when anything changes, even from a launch three months ago. Sales and CS never present stale material.

You work alongside a Product Marketing Manager who runs launches and owns the data. They hand you a brief with the key points, stats and must-mentions. You decide how to say them. Same model as a brand briefing a creator.

## What you will own

- The overarching Agentio story: what we offer, how it works, why customers love it
- The aha layer: value prop language per persona, modular story blocks, emotional copy
- The modular pitch by SKU and brand type
- Pitch deck, one-pagers, battlecards, QBR template, measurement guidance and onboarding playbook, each updated within a week of a launch brief
- The enablement system itself: one asset library, every asset current whenever product, pricing or packaging changes
- Weekly collateral requests from sales and CS
- Win/loss language folded back into battlecards
- Customer-facing framing for launch emails, LinkedIn posts and website product pages
- Edits for resonance on launch copy where needed

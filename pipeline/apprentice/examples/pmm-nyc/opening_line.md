# The opening line

The one sentence the student leads with when a Level 2 card goes somewhere ("<Company> has shown X. I want to help it Y, so I built Z."):

> Agentio has shown that a Shopify brand can see the creator-driven orders its pixel never caught, through the Fairing integration it shipped on 7 October. I want to help its sales team say that in one page a performance marketer forwards, so I built the one-pager from the launch post, with the brief it came from on the back.

For the battlecard: "Agentio has shown it can price creator ads on engaged views within a week of YouTube changing the count. I want to help its reps answer the two alternatives a brand can read about in public, so I built the battlecard against YouTube Creator Partnerships and Collabstr from their own pages."

For the explainer: "Agentio has shown what AI-native means in its own post. I want to help a CMO forward that in 300 words without a claim that will not survive, so I built the product-page section and the launch email."

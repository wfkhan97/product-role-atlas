Climb the ladder

Four sentences describe one skill at four levels: Entry, Mid, Senior and Lead. Put each sentence on its rung. Five skills a game. It is the clearest way we know to see what senior actually means.

Pick a field

We pick five skills its postings ask for. The number is how many of its skills have all four rungs written.

Your answers stay in this browser.

How a round works

  1. You get one skill and four sentences about it, shuffled.
  2. Drag each sentence onto its rung. Or select it and press 1–4.
  3. Check. The right rung earns 2 points, one rung off earns 1.
  4. See what changes as you move up, then the next skill.

Five skills, up to 40 points. Your score becomes a title, from Summer intern to Head of the ladder.

Where the sentences come from: 64 ladders written by the atlas for skills every field shares, and 16 drawn from postings, each for one industry (11 corporate, 4 bank, 1 consulting). Each round says which.
